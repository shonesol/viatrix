1 Who We Are

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, the Company is the data controller of the personal data described in this policy.

2 What Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Identity & contact: Full name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, country of residence, preferred language.
  • Booking details: Tour selection, travel dates, number of guests, special requests, dietary or accessibility needs.
  • Payment-related: Payment method chosen, transaction references, and limited billing details (we do not store full card numbers on our own servers when payments are processed by third-party gateways).
  • Communications: Messages you send via contact forms, email, WhatsApp, or review forms.
  • Reviews: Name, rating, tour reference, and review text if you submit feedback.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited (e.g. via standard web server logs or analytics tools, if enabled).
  • Cookies or similar technologies if used on our website (see Cookies section below).

2.3 Information from third parties

We may receive limited information from payment providers (e.g. payment confirmation) or from partners involved in delivering your tour (e.g. lodges confirming guest names), only as needed to fulfil your booking.

3 How We Use Your Data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide our services — process bookings, send confirmations and invoices, arrange logistics, and communicate about your tour.
  • To respond to enquiries — answer questions via email, WhatsApp, or the website.
  • To improve our services — analyse feedback and website use to improve itineraries and customer experience.
  • Marketing — where you have opted in or where permitted by law, send you information about tours and offers. You may opt out at any time.
  • Legal and safety — comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, and protect the rights and safety of clients and staff.
Legal bases (where applicable): performance of a contract; legitimate interests (e.g. improving services, security); consent (e.g. optional marketing); and legal obligation.

4 Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only as follows:

  • Service providers — accommodation, transport, park authorities, payment processors, and email/IT providers, solely as needed to deliver your booking or operate our business.
  • Professional advisers — lawyers, accountants, or insurers where necessary.
  • Authorities — when required by law, regulation, or court order, or to protect vital interests.
  • Business transfers — if the Company is involved in a merger or sale, data may be transferred under appropriate safeguards.

When we share data with providers outside your country, we take reasonable steps to ensure an adequate level of protection, consistent with applicable law.

5 Data Retention

  • Booking records — kept for as long as needed to fulfil the contract and for a reasonable period thereafter for accounting, tax, and dispute resolution (typically up to 7 years where required by law).
  • Enquiries & reviews — kept for as long as useful to respond and improve services, or until you request deletion where applicable.
  • Marketing lists — until you unsubscribe or we decide the data is no longer needed.

When data is no longer required, we will delete or anonymise it in a secure manner.

6 Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion in certain circumstances.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit processing in certain cases.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests or to direct marketing.
  • Data portability — receive data you provided in a structured, commonly used format, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at viatrixtoursandtravelco@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We aim to respond within a reasonable period (and within any time limits set by law).

7 Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. These include limited staff access, secure communication channels where practical, and careful selection of service providers.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please use official Company email and phone numbers only, and do not send full payment card details by unencrypted email.

8 Cookies & Website

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, preferences, and (if enabled) analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect site functionality.

Third-party services (e.g. embedded maps, payment pages, or analytics) may set their own cookies subject to their privacy policies.

9 Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16 without parental involvement. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 without consent from a parent or guardian. If you believe we have collected such data in error, please contact us so we can delete it.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Significant changes may also be communicated by email where appropriate. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy, where permitted by law.
